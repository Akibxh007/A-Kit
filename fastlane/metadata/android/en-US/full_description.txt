A-Kit is a free, open-source, offline-first utility suite built with Kotlin and Jetpack Compose for Android.

Key Features:
• Real-time Battery Telemetry: Monitor live charging wattage, voltage, temperature, current, and estimated battery runtime.
• AMOLED Always-On Display (AOD): Customizable charging and lockscreen AOD styles with Material You aesthetics, live gauges, and quick shortcuts.
• Drop Wi-Fi Share: Fast, offline peer-to-peer file sharing over local hotspot without needing internet or cloud uploads.
• SnapClean: Quickly scan, review, and clean up old screenshots to free up device storage.
• Thermal Monitor: Keep track of device temperature trends in real time.

Privacy First:
A-Kit collects zero personal data, contains no ads or trackers, and operates completely offline.
